# Tower Bugs v0.2.4 Balance

- Level 1 Armor Tier 1 remains visually armored but is specifically balanced for Fly-only progression.
- Intro armor HP is 72% of the prior value, defense is capped at 0.75, and movement speed is reduced by 10%.
- Level 1 Wave 5 contains 11 total frogs, including its boss.
- Level 1 Wave 5 regular frogs receive reduced HP, speed, and defense and do not receive path-pressure speed boosts.
- Bosses remain completely non-attacking and only advance toward the Tower.
- First Bug Buck training remains 3 Bug Bucks.
